Initial release on F-Droid! 🎉

Alpha GPS geotags your photos by sending your phone's GPS to Sony Alpha cameras over Bluetooth LE, and doubles as a remote shutter. This build is fully FOSS: no Google Play Services, no tracking.

Also new in v1.5.0:
- Remote shutter: full capture sequence (focus, shoot, release) driven by camera status
- Prompt to put the camera into pairing mode when needed
- Vietnamese translation
- Bug fixes and dependency updates
